Professional Profile

Udhab Dahal is a legal professional and management expert based in Kathmandu, Nepal. With more than 12 years of experience, his work focuses on legal consultation, business formation, liquidation and organizational management.

His professional interests include civil and criminal law, corporate legal matters, cooperative organizations, and the legal and management challenges faced by businesses and institutions.

He has handled more than 1,000 cases and has experience providing legal advisory services to cooperatives. His professional experience also includes involvement in more than 10 public interest litigation (PIL) cases in the Supreme Court and work associated with the High Court in Patan.

As a lawyer and management expert, he works in areas including business formation, liquidation, and legal matters concerning NGOs and INGOs.

He holds an LLB and an MBS degree and has professional qualifications in liquidation. He has also published research papers related to cooperatives and business management.

His goal is to provide clear, responsible and practical legal support while maintaining transparency, professionalism and high ethical standards.
